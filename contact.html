Helpful Information to Include

To help our agricultural experts provide better advice, please share:

  • Your crop name, variety and current growth stage
  • Your farming location and planting date
  • Soil type and irrigation method
  • Symptoms, visible damage and when the problem started
  • Recent fertilizer, pesticide or other treatments used
  • Weather conditions, rainfall or unusual temperature changes
  • Estimated area of the field affected
  • Clear photos of affected leaves, stems, roots or fruits
  • Any previous attempts to solve the problem

Tip: Clear photos and accurate crop information can help experts assess your farming problem more effectively.

Note: Official contact details should be added after verifying the relevant extension office's current phone number and email address.
