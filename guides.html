Farming Guides

Practical tutorials to help you make informed farming decisions.

Rice • Kharif

Rice Cultivation Guide

Understand seed selection, nursery preparation, transplanting and water management.

Steps to follow
  1. Select a suitable variety for the local season.
  2. Prepare a level field and healthy seedlings.
  3. Transplant at the recommended spacing.
  4. Manage water and nutrients based on crop stage.
  5. Monitor pests and harvest at maturity.

Tip: Follow local agricultural extension recommendations for variety and planting dates.

Wheat • Rabi

Wheat Production

Learn about seedbed preparation, sowing, nutrient management and harvesting.

Steps to follow
  1. Choose an adapted, quality seed variety.
  2. Prepare a fine, well-drained seedbed.
  3. Sow at the recommended depth and spacing.
  4. Manage irrigation and fertilizer according to soil tests.
  5. Harvest when grain reaches suitable maturity.

Tip: Avoid waterlogging and use locally recommended sowing dates.

Vegetables • Organic

Organic Vegetable Farming

Build healthy soil and reduce unnecessary chemical inputs.

Steps to follow
  1. Select crops suited to the climate and season.
  2. Add mature compost based on soil needs.
  3. Use healthy seeds and appropriate spacing.
  4. Apply mulch and efficient irrigation.
  5. Scout for pests and use IPM methods.

Tip: Use only approved inputs and properly composted organic matter.

Fruits • Orchard

Fruit Tree Management

Learn how to establish and maintain productive fruit trees.

Steps to follow
  1. Choose healthy planting material.
  2. Prepare a suitable planting location and pit.
  3. Plant at the locally recommended spacing.
  4. Prune appropriately and monitor nutrition.
  5. Harvest carefully at the right maturity.

Tip: Pruning and planting times depend on the fruit species.

Irrigation • Modern

Efficient Water Management

Use water wisely while meeting the needs of growing crops.

Steps to follow
  1. Check soil moisture before irrigation.
  2. Understand crop water requirements.
  3. Repair leaks and maintain irrigation equipment.
  4. Water at appropriate times.
  5. Check for drainage and waterlogging.

Tip: Irrigation needs vary with soil, weather and crop stage.

Soil • Traditional

Soil Preparation

Prepare productive soil while protecting its long-term structure.

Steps to follow
  1. Inspect the field and test the soil.
  2. Remove problematic weeds appropriately.
  3. Improve drainage where needed.
  4. Incorporate suitable organic matter.
  5. Prepare the seedbed without excessive tillage.

Tip: Soil tests help avoid unnecessary fertilizer use.
